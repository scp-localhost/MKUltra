## Instructions

1. For each practice item, read the response text carefully.
2. Score ONLY the primary trait cluster listed (4–5 dimensions).
3. Write your scores on paper or in a separate file. Do NOT look at the key.
4. After scoring all 6 items, read the scoring key for each.
5. For any dimension where your score differs from the key by more than 0.3:
   - Re-read the relevant trait entry in `docs/data_dictionary.md`
   - Re-read the anchor examples in `docs/data_dictionary_expansion_v1_1.md`
6. If you still disagree with the key after re-reading, flag it and contact König.
   Do not proceed to the real subsample with unresolved calibration disagreements.

**Passing criterion:** No more than 2 dimensions differing by > 0.3 across the
full 6-item set. If you have 3 or more, re-read the dictionary and repeat.

**Important:** These are synthetic responses written specifically for calibration.
They are deliberately unambiguous on the primary cluster. The real data will be
more ambiguous — that is expected. Practice items are designed for training, not
for realistic difficulty.
