# Paper 5 — Measuring Identity Drift in LLM Systems

## Core Claim
Identity drift in LLMs under persona injection can be operationalized and quantified using behavioral consistency metrics derived from analogical human frameworks.

## Problem
Current discussions of LLM behavioral drift are descriptive and anecdotal.
There is no standardized metric for:
- Stability of persona adherence
- Magnitude of deviation over time
- Cross-context behavioral consistency

## Contribution
This paper introduces a **Behavioral Stability Index (BSI)**:
- Quantifies drift across interaction sequences
- Tracks deviation from baseline identity constraints
- Enables comparative evaluation of prompt interventions

## Method Stack
- Input: Controlled prompt sequences with identity anchors
- Output: Response embeddings + trait extraction
- Metrics:
  - Semantic drift (embedding distance over turns)
  - Trait consistency (alignment with archetype schema)
  - Authority compliance gradient (Milgram-inspired scaling)

## Experimental Design
- Baseline neutral model vs injected persona conditions
- Multi-turn conversational trials
- Cross-domain prompts (ethical, emotional, technical)

## Expected Outcome
Demonstration that:
- Persona injection produces measurable drift signatures
- Drift follows identifiable trajectories (not random noise)
- Stability varies by archetype class

## Role in Portfolio
This paper provides **quantitative legitimacy** to Papers 1–4.
It is the “you can measure it” defense layer for the exegesis.
