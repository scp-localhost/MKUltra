# Paper 7 — Parallel Failure Modes in Humans and LLMs

## Core Claim
LLM identity drift under persona injection exhibits structural parallels to human susceptibility to authority, framing, and social engineering.

## Problem
Analogies between human and LLM behavior are often dismissed as metaphorical rather than structural.

## Contribution
This paper formalizes **cross-domain behavioral equivalence**:
- Authority gradient → model deference patterns
- Social engineering tactics → prompt injection strategies
- Cognitive bias analogs → token probability distortions

## Method
- Map known human experiments (Milgram, Cialdini) to LLM scenarios
- Replicate conditions in prompt-based experiments
- Compare response patterns

## Expected Outcome
- Structural similarities emerge in compliance behavior
- Differences highlight limits of analogy (important for viva defense)

## Role in Portfolio
This paper strengthens the **exegesis argument**:
You are not claiming LLMs are human —
You are proving they share *manipulable behavioral surfaces*.
